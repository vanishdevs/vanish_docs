# 📍 Live Location

A custom lb-phone app, styled after Find My, that lets players share their live location with people in their phone contacts.

## Features

* Installs straight into lb-phone as its own app, no extra app store setup needed,
* Players send a share request to a contact, who can accept or decline it,
* Once accepted, both sides see each other move on the map in real time,
* Either side can revoke a share at any time,
* A map blip points to contacts who are sharing with you,
* Online and "last seen" indicators so you know whether a location is live or stale,
* Built to stay light on busy servers, with tunable push rate, movement thresholds and presence checks,
* Optional logging of share requests, accepts, declines and revokes to Discord, Fivemanage, ox\_lib or a custom handler,
* Works alongside ESX, QB and QBOX
